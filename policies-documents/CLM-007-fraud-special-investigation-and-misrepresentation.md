## 1. Purpose

Most claims are honest. Some contain mistakes, some contain exaggeration, and a smaller number contain deliberate misrepresentation. This policy tells claim staff how to recognize a concern, how to refer it, and how to keep handling the honest parts of a file while the Special Investigation Unit does its work. It also tells SIU what it may decide and what must go to Legal or to a claim manager before the customer hears the word "fraud."

Northline does not use fraud procedures to win ordinary coverage disputes. A weak claim, a confused inventory, or a customer who is angry is not a fraud file. Mislabeling it as one injures the customer and, because regulators read these files, injures the Company. The reverse is also true. A file with a serious, specific inconsistency is not kept on the ordinary desk because the adjuster is uncomfortable making a referral.

This policy is an internal control. It is not a description of techniques for committing fraud, and it is not a set of scripts for deceiving a claimant. Staff follow it and stop.

## 2. Scope

Every Northline employee and every vendor adjuster who handles a Northline claim must refer concerns as this policy requires. SIU accepts referrals on first-party and third-party claims. SIU does not investigate employees except through the separate corporate investigations process, and it does not investigate producers except together with Agency Management and Compliance. A claim that also involves a suspected dishonest employee is referred to both places the day the employee issue appears. The claim referral does not wait for the employee investigation to finish.

Law-enforcement reporting is decided by SIU leadership with Legal, under Section 10. Adjusters do not call a police department to report a customer for fraud on their own authority.

## 3. Definitions

**Material misrepresentation.** A false statement of fact, made in connection with the claim, that a reasonable insurer would consider important to coverage or to the amount payable. An error the customer corrects when asked, a difference of opinion about value, and a statement that is wrong but unimportant to the result are not material misrepresentation under this policy.

**Concealment.** A deliberate withholding of a fact the policy or a specific question required the person to disclose. Failure to volunteer a fact nobody asked about is evaluated under the policy wording and the law. It is not automatically concealment.

**SIU hold.** A system status that stops indemnity payment until a manager releases it. It does not stop make-safe work that prevents further damage, and it does not stop a defense that CLM-002 requires, unless Coverage Counsel approves a different course.

**Referral.** A written transfer of a concern to SIU with the facts that prompted it. A referral is not an accusation. The customer is not told "you have been referred for fraud."

**Indicator.** A fact that, alone or with others, justifies a referral. Indicators are not proof. Proof is SIU's conclusion after an investigation that meets Section 7.

## 4. Governing principles

1. Refer facts, not hunches about the kind of person the customer is. Nationality, neighborhood, accent, and demeanor are not indicators and are not written in the referral as if they were.
2. The ordinary claim continues. Undisputed amounts that are unrelated to the concern are paid unless a manager documents why payment would compromise the investigation.
3. Staff do not entrap, do not invent a pretext for a call, and do not secretly record a conversation. Recorded statements follow CLM-003.
4. The customer is not accused in a note, a letter, or a conversation unless a fraud denial has been approved under Section 9 and CLM-006.
5. Mistakes are allowed to be mistakes. The first response to an inconsistency is usually a plain question.
6. SIU independence matters. The unit reports to the Head of Special Investigation, not to the desk that wants a denial. Claim management may not order a particular SIU outcome.
7. Confidentiality is tight. Referral contents are not discussed with agents, underwriters, or other claimants beyond what that function must know. Underwriting may be told the claim outcome after it is final, through the ordinary underwriting-referral path, not through informal comment.

## 5. What the front line does first

### 5.1 Ask before referring, when it is safe to ask

When a single fact does not match — a date, a mileage, an item on an inventory — the adjuster asks the customer for an explanation in a normal claim question. "The shop report shows 62,000 miles and the application shows 41,000. Can you help me reconcile that?" is the right tone. The customer's explanation is written down. If the explanation resolves the point, there is no referral and the note says the point was resolved. If the explanation creates a larger inconsistency, or if the customer refuses to address a specific material point, the adjuster refers.

The adjuster does not ask a series of trick questions designed to produce a contradiction. The adjuster does not confront a customer with an accusation to "see how they react." Reaction is not evidence.

### 5.2 Indicators that require a referral

The adjuster refers the file the same business day any of the following is present. The referral does not wait for the adjuster to build a case.

- A document appears altered, or two official documents state inconsistent material facts and the customer, after one plain question, cannot account for the difference.
- The customer asks Northline to pay for property that a prior inspection, a photo, or an independent record shows was not damaged in this event, and the dollar amount is material.
- A bodily-injury claim describes a mechanism that the medical records contradict in a way that is not a matter of medical opinion — for example, treatment records dated before the alleged accident — after the dates have been checked for a clerical error.
- The same property or the same injury is claimed with another insurer for the same event and the customer denied the other claim when asked.
- A fire or theft loss in which the official report and the claim statement conflict on a material point such as whether the premises were secured or whether the insured was present.
- An insured, a runner, or a provider has already been the subject of a confirmed SIU finding on another Northline file, and the current file shares a material feature with that finding. The mere fact that someone had a prior honest claim is not this indicator.
- A lawyer, medical provider, or body shop is on the current SIU watch list issued to claim staff. The watch list is used only for referrals. It is not announced to customers and it is not a reason to deny a claim by itself.
- An employee believes they are being asked to participate in a false claim, including by another employee or an agent.

A large claim, a claim from a new policyholder, a claim shortly after inception, or a claim with a public adjuster is not, by itself, a referral indicator. Those facts may explain why the adjuster looks carefully at the ordinary proof. They do not, alone, go to SIU. Sending every early-tenure claim to SIU is a practice defect and will be reversed on audit.

### 5.3 What the adjuster may still do

Until SIU or a manager says otherwise, the adjuster continues the investigation under CLM-003, keeps the reserve honest under CLM-004, and pays undisputed unrelated amounts under CLM-005. The adjuster does not delay a make-safe vendor because a referral is open. The adjuster does not tell a vendor, an agent, or a neighbor that SIU is involved.

## 6. How to refer

The referral is the NCF form. It contains the claim number, the specific indicator, the documents that show it, what the customer has already been asked, and what the customer said. It does not contain a recommended denial and it does not contain jokes or character comments. The adjuster selects "payment hold requested" only when paying the disputed portion now would make recovery unrealistic, and a supervisor confirms the hold the same day. Holds are reviewed by SIU within two business days. A hold that SIU does not confirm is released.

If the system is unavailable, the adjuster emails the SIU intake box with the same content and copies the supervisor. Personal email accounts are not used.

SIU acknowledges the referral within two business days and either accepts it, declines it with a reason, or asks for one missing document. A declined referral is returned to ordinary handling. The adjuster does not re-refer the same fact unless a new indicator appears. Repeat referrals of a declined point are themselves reviewed by the claim manager and the Head of Special Investigation.

## 7. SIU investigation

### 7.1 Scope of the work

SIU investigates the referred concern. It does not reopen every part of the customer's life. Inquiries are tied to the claim: the occurrence, the property, the injury, the other insurance, and the statements made in the claim. SIU may ask the adjuster to obtain documents through the ordinary policy duties. SIU may take an examination under oath only when CLM-003's predicates are met and a claim manager concurs. SIU may not invent a different predicate.

Surveillance, if used at all, is approved by the Head of Special Investigation and, if the claim is in suit, by the litigation supervisor. It is limited to activity that the injury or disability claim has put in issue. It is stopped when the question is answered. Reports describe what was observed. They do not diagnose the person. Adjusters are not shown raw surveillance as entertainment, and surveillance is not discussed with anyone who does not need it to evaluate the claim.

### 7.2 Contact with the customer

SIU may interview the customer with the same notice rules as any recorded statement. SIU identifies itself honestly. It does not pose as a vendor, a neighbor, or a regulator. If the customer is represented, contact goes through the representative. If the customer asks whether they are being investigated, the answer is truthful at the level Legal has approved for that stage of the file: usually that Northline is reviewing a specific inconsistency, which is then described. Staff do not say "no" when the answer is yes.

### 7.3 Timing

SIU completes the first assessment within fifteen business days of accepting a referral on a standard file, and within five business days if a payment hold is in place. The assessment states whether the concern is unsupported, still open, or supported. An open concern has a written plan and a predicted end date. A hold is not allowed to run past thirty days without the Head of Special Investigation's approval and a letter to the customer, approved by a claim manager, that explains the specific information still needed. The letter does not accuse. A silent hold is a service failure and, on a first-party file, a regulatory concern.

## 8. Findings

SIU writes one of four findings:

| Finding | Meaning | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsupported | The indicator was resolved or was never material. | Hold released the same day. Ordinary handling. The referral is not mentioned in customer letters. |
| Mistake | The fact was wrong and the customer corrected it or the records show an error without an intent to mislead. | The claim is adjusted to the true facts. No fraud language. |
| Valuation dispute | The parties disagree about price or cause, and the disagreement is not a false statement of fact. | Returned to the desk for CLM-004 and, if needed, CLM-009. Not a fraud denial. |
| Supported material misrepresentation | SIU states the false fact, the evidence, why it is material, and why the evidence shows the customer knew it was false or was reckless in the way the governing law requires. | A claim manager and Coverage Counsel decide the remedy under Section 9. |

SIU does not send the denial letter. SIU does not negotiate the settlement. SIU does not threaten criminal action to obtain a lower settlement. A finding is not softened or hardened because of the size of the loss or the identity of the agent.

## 9. Remedies

The remedy follows the policy and the law, not the anger the file provokes.

- If the misrepresentation affects only a part of the amount, and the policy and the law allow severance, the false portion is removed and the rest is paid. The letter is a partial denial under CLM-006 and uses fraud language only with Legal's approval.
- If the policy's concealment clause and the governing law defeat the entire claim, a full denial may be approved. The letter states the facts and the clause. It is approved under the fraud-denial row of CLM-006.
- Criminal referral is a separate decision under Section 10. It is not required in order to deny, and a denial is not required in order to refer. They are not used as leverage against each other.
- Restitution for money already paid is requested in writing. Payment plans may be accepted by a claim manager. A confession of judgment or a sweeping release of unrelated rights is not demanded as the price of closing an SIU file.

A customer who disagrees with the finding uses the appeal path in the denial letter and CLM-009. SIU does not run its own appeal. The second reviewer has access to the SIU file and is not bound by it if the evidence does not support the finding.

## 10. Law enforcement and civil recovery

Northline reports suspected insurance fraud to the appropriate state fraud bureau or law-enforcement agency when the law requires a report, and it may report when the law permits a report and Legal agrees the evidence meets the Company's reporting standard. The standard is a supported finding of deliberate material misrepresentation, not a mere referral. Reports are factual, include the documents Legal specifies, and are sent by SIU leadership. Copies are kept in a restricted file. The customer is not threatened with a report to induce a settlement. If a report has been made and the customer asks, Legal decides what may be said.

Civil recovery against a third party who participated in a false claim — a shop, a provider, or an organizer — is Legal's decision. Claim staff do not invoice those parties on their own. Any recovery is coded so it does not distort the claim reserve.

Employees who are contacted by law enforcement about a claim tell SIU and Legal the same day and do not give informal statements about the file. They may acknowledge that they handled the claim. They do not speculate.

## 11. Vendor and provider concerns

A body shop, mitigation company, medical provider, or public adjuster whose bills appear systematically unrelated to the damage or the injury may be referred once, with examples from more than a single line item on a single file. SIU and Legal decide whether the issue is a claim dispute, a vendor-audit matter, or a reportable fraud matter. Until that decision, the vendor remains subject to ordinary estimate review under CLM-004. Staff do not blacklist a vendor in conversation, and they do not tell a customer which shops to avoid beyond the approved-network rules. Removal of a vendor from an approved network is a Vendor Management decision with a written reason.

## 12. Documentation, access, and retention

SIU notes are stored in the restricted section of NCF. Adjusters see the finding and the instructions that affect payment. They do not see investigative work product that Legal has marked restricted, except as needed for a denial letter's factual basis, which must be supportable from documents the letter relies on. A denial cannot rest on a secret file the customer can never test. If a fact is too sensitive to state, it is not used as a basis for the denial until Legal finds a lawful way to rely on it.

Access to the restricted section is logged. Browsing SIU files without an assignment is a conduct violation. Retention follows the Records Schedule. A legal hold stops destruction. Closing the claim does not authorize deletion of the SIU record ahead of the schedule.

## 13. Quality, training, and protections for staff

Quality Review and SIU audit a sample of referrals each quarter. They look for referrals based on protected characteristics or on mere suspicion, for indicators that were not referred, for holds that ran in silence, and for denial letters that outran the finding. Results go to the Claims Governance Committee. A desk with almost no referrals and a desk that refers a large share of its files are both examined. Either pattern can be a problem.

New adjusters complete the referral module before they handle files alone. The module uses examples of resolved inconsistencies that should not be referred and examples of documents that must be referred. SIU staff complete legal training on interviews, holds, and reporting before they accept files. Annual refreshers are mandatory.

Retaliation against an employee who refers a concern, including a concern about another employee or an agent, is prohibited. So is pressure to withdraw a referral. An employee who believes they were punished for a good-faith referral reports it to Compliance. The referral itself does not need to have been correct, only made in good faith on facts.

## 14. Exceptions

There is no exception that allows an adjuster to accuse a customer of fraud in a letter because a manager is unavailable. There is no exception that allows a silent payment hold beyond the times in Section 7.3. A CAT bulletin may extend SIU's first-assessment time if volume makes the standard impossible, and it must say so in writing. It may not extend a payment hold without the customer letter Section 7.3 requires. Immediate danger — a person altering a scene while on the phone, a threat — is handled by stopping the contact and calling a supervisor and, if needed, law enforcement for the threat. That call is about safety, not about fraud reporting.

## 15. Related policies

CLM-001 and CLM-003 govern statements and documents. CLM-002 governs the concealment condition as a coverage term once SIU has a finding. CLM-004 keeps reserves accurate while a referral is open; a referral is not a reason to drop a reserve to zero or to inflate it. CLM-005 governs payment of undisputed amounts and recovery of overpayments. CLM-006 governs the denial letter. CLM-008 is not used to "recover" from an insured by calling ordinary salvage subrogation. CLM-009 is the customer's appeal. CLM-010 may adjust SIU timing in a catastrophe and does not change the definition of material misrepresentation.

## Appendix A — Examples

**Mileage difference.** The application shows 41,000 miles and the shop writes 62,000. The adjuster asks the customer. The customer says they bought the car used, the application mileage was an estimate the agent typed, and they can send the purchase papers. The papers match 60,000 miles at purchase. This is an application-accuracy issue for Underwriting, not a claim-fraud referral, unless the customer changed the story after being shown the papers. The claim is handled on the true mileage. Underwriting is notified through the ordinary path.

**Inventory item that was not there.** After a theft, the insured lists a camera. A photo from a prior claim three months earlier shows the same camera already described as destroyed and paid. The adjuster asks, once, in plain language. The insured says it was a different camera and then cannot describe either. The adjuster refers with both photos. The adjuster does not write "insured is a fraud." SIU examines materiality and knowledge. The rest of the inventory is still evaluated. Amounts that are not in question are not held without a confirmed hold.

**Early claim that is real.** A policy is thirty days old. A kitchen pipe breaks. The plumber's report, photos, and water pattern match a sudden break. The adjuster handles the claim under CLM-002 and CLM-004. A referral that says only "new policy" is declined and is recorded as an improper referral for coaching, not as a mark against the customer.

**Dates in medical records.** A claimant says the collision was 2 May. The first treatment note is dated 2 April and describes the same collision. The adjuster checks for a typographical error with the provider's billing office through the ordinary authorization. The provider confirms the April date is correct and the claimant then says they "must have mixed up the month" while the wage form still claims the May date. The adjuster refers. The referral includes the confirmation. It does not include a comment on the claimant's character.

**Hold too quiet.** SIU accepts a referral and the supervisor confirms a hold on the disputed jewelry portion of a homeowners claim. Twenty days pass with no letter. The dwelling payment, which is unrelated and undisputed, was correctly issued. The jewelry hold is now a problem. SIU and the claim manager send the Section 7.3 letter the same day they discover the silence, or they release the hold if the concern cannot be stated specifically enough to write the letter honestly.

**Valuation, not fraud.** A public adjuster prices a roof at $48,000. Northline's measured estimate is $27,000. The difference is unit cost and a matching line. There is no false statement of what is on the roof. This is a valuation dispute under CLM-004 and, if needed, appraisal under CLM-009. An SIU referral that says "public adjuster is inflated" without a false fact is declined.

**Employee pressure.** An agent tells an adjuster to "keep this one off SIU, the insured is a friend of the office." The adjuster refers if an indicator is present and reports the agent's request to Agency Management and Compliance the same day. The agent's request is the conduct issue. It is not an indicator against the insured by itself.

## Appendix B — Referral checklist

- [ ] The indicator is a fact, and the fact is described specifically.
- [ ] A single inconsistency was the subject of one plain question first, unless asking would risk the loss of evidence or someone's safety.
- [ ] The customer's answer is in the referral.
- [ ] No comment relies on appearance, neighborhood, language, or demeanor as an indicator.
- [ ] A payment hold was requested only for the disputed portion and is confirmed by a supervisor.
- [ ] Make-safe and unrelated undisputed payments are still proceeding.
- [ ] The customer has not been told they are "being investigated for fraud."
- [ ] The referral went through NCF or the SIU intake box, not a personal inbox.
- [ ] Agents and vendors have not been told that SIU is involved.
- [ ] If a prior SIU finding is the indicator, the shared feature is named. A prior honest claim is not cited.
